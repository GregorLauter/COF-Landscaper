# Example data sources

## ILCOF-1

Rabbani et al. (2013) reported the original framework. [Original publication](https://doi.org/10.1002/chem.201203753)

The experimental PXRD data were provided by Eva Dahlqvist (Chalmers University of Technology, Sweden). The experimental wavelength was 1.5406 Å.

- [Default example](main_workflow/local/default/experimental_pxrd/ILCOF-1.xy)
- [Configurable example](main_workflow/local/configurable/experimental_pxrd/ILCOF-1.xy)
- [HPC example](main_workflow/hpc/experimental_pxrd/ILCOF-1.xy)

## COF-LZU-1

Ding et al. (2011) reported the original framework. [Original publication](https://doi.org/10.1021/ja206846p)

The experimental PXRD data were provided by Eva Dahlqvist (Chalmers University of Technology, Sweden). The experimental wavelength was 1.5406 Å.

- [Scaling example](additional/scaling/experimental_pxrd/COF-LZU-1_exp.xy)

## BP-COF-1

Gropp et al. (2020) reported the original framework. [Original publication](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.abd6406)

The framework is constructed from manually prepared molecular fragments. No experimental PXRD data are used in this example.
